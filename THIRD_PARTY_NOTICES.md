## Other assets

- Geist is self-hosted through `@fontsource-variable/geist` under the SIL Open Font License included in that package.
- Lucide icons are supplied by `lucide-react` under its ISC licence. The company tiles and credential marks are typographic identifiers, not official achievement badges.
- Qasd screenshots are from the owner's public `Karim-codes/qasd` repository, `docs/previews/home-390.png` and `counter-390.png`. Its documentation identifies the itinerary as synthetic demonstration data. They are actual exported application screens, not recreated mockups.
- The cloud architecture is an original schematic of the lab described in the supplied brief. The VM, VNet and NSG icons are unmodified official Microsoft Azure architecture icons from the V25 collection. Microsoft permits copying, distribution and display for architecture diagrams, training materials and documentation; all other rights are reserved. Source and terms: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/icons/ . The small Azure identifier is a simplified brand mark. No Microsoft endorsement is implied.
- Microsoft colour tiles are used only to identify the certification issuer. No verification URLs or credential IDs have been invented.

- Shaanis screenshots were copied read-only from the owner’s local `Somali-Turub-Shaanis/docs/screenshots/home.png` and `table.png`. They are actual Flutter widget renders; the table uses an offline test fixture with example players. Original PNGs remain in `content/assets/`; WebP copies only change encoding and resolution. No online room codes, live credentials or private source were copied.

- iTinyri product images come from its public UK App Store listing (`id6754219093`), using the screenshot URLs returned by Apple’s public lookup API. They are the owner’s published product images, not reconstructed interfaces. Original JPEGs remain in `content/assets/`.
- PolyGlotPDF’s product image is an actual anonymous headless-browser capture of https://www.polyglotpdf.com/ on 26 November 2026. The crop contains the header, introduction and upload area; usage/rating counters and browser chrome are outside the crop. No documents were uploaded and no account was accessed.
